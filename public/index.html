💡 Swing ideas new setups from daily charts

Rule-based setups from daily prices: pullback (uptrend that dipped to its 20/50-day average, RSI 38–55), breakout (close above the 55-day high on 1.5× volume, not stretched), oversold bounce (above the 200-day average, RSI ≤ 35) and value turning up (the app's buy zone + price back above its 20-day average). Score adds trend, strength vs Nifty, risk:reward, valuation and liquidity. Size = no more than your max per trade, and a stop-out costs at most your risk % of swing cash. A screen to find candidates, not advice: check the chart and news before you buy, and always use the stop.
